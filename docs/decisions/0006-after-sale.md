# ADR 0006: Delivery, shipments, returns, credit notes and refunds (Phase 4b)

Status: accepted 2026-11-21. `Plan/Phase4b-Design-Proposal.md` approved as written ("Approve, start coding"). The open questions had no separate answers, so these were decided while building (all can be changed):

1. **Outside every zone:** not a hard stop. Staff with `sales.discount` (Admin) can deliver with a typed charge and a reason.
2. **Credit notes:** a return always issues its own credit note, so Cashiers (`sales.return`) get one automatically. Credit notes with no goods coming back (price adjustment, goodwill, delivery) need the new key `creditnotes.issue` (Admin).
3. **Restock cost:** the quantity-weighted cost the line was **issued** at, not today's average. Stock value reverses exactly.
4. **Carriers:** free text, with NZ Post, CourierPost, Mainfreight and Own van suggested.
5. **Zones:** none seeded. Until an active zone exists, the 4a behaviour stays: staff type the delivery charge.

## Decisions

- **Delivery settings** are one row: delivery on/off, pickup on/off, shop-wide free-delivery threshold. The GST status is read-only: not registered.
- **Zones** have a charge, an optional free-over threshold (overrides the shop one), a rural surcharge, a service promise and 4-digit postcode ranges. Overlapping ranges between active zones are rejected on save, so a postcode has one quote.
- **Quote** = zone charge, or 0 when the goods value after discounts reaches the threshold, plus the rural surcharge (free delivery covers the standard run only). The order input now carries `DeliveryPostcode`, `DeliveryIsRural` and `DeliveryChargeReason`. The server quotes when the draft is saved. A different charge needs a reason, and a lower one also needs `sales.discount`. The order keeps a snapshot of the postcode, rural flag, zone name, promise and reason, frozen at Confirm like the rest of the invoice. Editing a zone never changes an order. The preview returns the quote so the editor can show it.
- **Shipments** (`SHP-`): Pending → Dispatched → Delivered, or Pending → Cancelled. Pending shipments hold their quantity, so two shipments cannot plan the same goods. **Dispatch posts the 4a stock issue** in the same transaction (`stock_issues.ShipmentId` is unique). It needs an idempotency key, and a retry returns the shipment unchanged. **Delivered changes nothing in stock.** A database trigger enforces the transitions and stamps the times. Counter pickups still use the plain *Issue stock*.
- **Returns** (`RTN-`): lines against the invoice, each with **Restock** (a `Return` movement at the issue cost) or **WriteOff** (no movement). The limit is issued − already returned. It is checked under the order-row lock and again by a deferred database trigger, so two racing returns cannot both take the last unit. Returns are append-only and idempotent.
- **Credit notes** (`CN-`, gapless): a return issues one automatically. Credit for returned units uses **cumulative rounding**: after *n* of *N* units have come back, the line has been credited round(line total × n / N) in total. Returning a whole line in any number of steps therefore credits exactly its line total, including its share of the order discount. Standalone credits are capped at the line total minus earlier credits. A delivery credit is capped at the delivery charge minus earlier delivery credits. Database triggers re-check both caps and that the lines add up to the note's total. `CreditTotal ≤ Total` is a check constraint. Credit notes are NotRegistered, with GST 0 and currency NZD. The PDF heading is "CREDIT NOTE", with "Not GST registered - no GST charged" and "not a cash refund".
- **Balance**: net total = total − credits, and net paid = received after voids − refunds paid out. Balance = net total − net paid (negative means the shop owes the customer). The payment status uses these. The invoice row keeps `CreditTotal` and `RefundedTotal`, which are the only new columns the confirmed-invoice guard lets change.
- **Refunds** (`RFD-`): Requested → Approved → Succeeded or Failed. Requested or Approved → Cancelled. `Processing` is kept for Phase 6 gateways.
  - Creating needs `refunds.create` (Cashier, Admin). Approving needs `refunds.approve`, and the approver must not be the requester (server check plus a check constraint). Paying out or failing needs `refunds.execute`, a method and an evidence reference. Cancelling needs `refunds.approve`.
  - **Limit** under the order-row lock: what the shop holds beyond what is owed (net paid − net total), minus refunds that are requested, approved or processing. For a **cancellation** before any issue the limit is the full net paid. It is re-checked at approval, in case a payment was voided in between. Racing requests cannot together exceed the limit.
  - Each refund is allocated to receipts, newest first, never beyond what a receipt has left. A deferred trigger checks the allocations. A receipt with a live refund cannot be voided.
  - Completing is idempotent on its own key. Only Succeeded adds to `RefundedTotal`, so an approved refund is not money paid out. Refunds never move stock.
- **Cancel with payments**: an invoice with nothing issued can be cancelled once its net paid is zero. That means voided, or refunded with `ForCancellation`. The database guard also refuses a cancel while net paid ≠ 0.
- **Daily takings**: refunds count on the shop day they are **paid out**, by method. Net = received − voided − refunded. Credit notes issued are shown as a total for reconciliation; they are not money. The CSV adds refunds as negative rows marked `refund`.
- **Permissions and menus**: new key `creditnotes.issue` (Admin). Existing keys are now used: `sales.return`, `delivery.configure`, `fulfillment.update`, `refunds.*`. Enabled menus: Sales → Returns, Credit notes, Refunds, Shipments, Delivery settings.
- **Not in 4b**: online checkout, gateway refunds and emailing documents (Phase 6).
